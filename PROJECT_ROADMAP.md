# Storm & Me Official — Product Roadmap

## Vision
Storm & Me is not only an author website. It is an interactive creative home where visitors can see and hear the storm, find hope after it, discover books and music that meet them where they are, and leave feeling lighter.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and Trust
- [x] Cinematic storm homepage
- [x] Rain, lightning, thunder, sunlight, haze, and subtle post-storm rainbow
- [x] Visible rain and slow-piano controls with a reliable weather-mode crossfade
- [x] Remove duplicate audio components and lock one source of truth
- [x] Add cache recovery and remove the temporary build marker after verification
- [x] Remove demo purchase behavior and use honest Amazon retailer links
- [x] Remove dead social and YouTube destinations until verified links are supplied
- [x] Remove fake music previews, fake videos, fake product launches, and false release posts
- [ ] Visually verify every book cover and Amazon destination on desktop and mobile
- [ ] Complete the final mobile and accessibility audit

## Phase 2 — Bookstore Experience
- [x] Organize books into healing, children’s, career/life, fiction, and Coming Soon shelves
- [x] Clearly label series order for The Last Block on Earth Books One and Two
- [x] Feature The Storm in Me as the heart of the mission
- [x] Give every book a rich landing-page foundation with description, author note, series guidance, Amazon purchase link, and conditional areas for samples, trailers, and real reviews
- [x] Add Coming Soon shelf for Let It Go, Little Bear and UNHAPPY
- [ ] Add approved sample chapters, trailers, quotes, and real reader reviews as supplied
- [ ] Add persistent email notification workflow for future releases

## Phase 3 — Author and Story
- [x] Rebuild the Meet Willy Will / Will Scott author page with the real portrait, biography, creative identities, values, and timeline
- [ ] Expand The Story Behind the Storm into a deeper long-form narrative
- [ ] Add behind-the-book stories and writing-process content

## Phase 4 — Music and Video

- [ ] Add official YouTube channel and video embeds
- [ ] Add documentaries, book trailers, lyric videos, and behind-the-scenes content

## Signature Experiences
- [x] Rotating homepage encouragement such as “Broken is not your final chapter.”
- [x] Storm mode with visual rain, lightning, thunder, and a gentle rain-audio option
- [x] After-the-storm mode with sunlight, a natural distant rainbow, and slow peaceful piano
- [x] A bookstore that feels like walking through a story rather than scrolling through one long product grid
- [x] Every completed element supports the message: the storm does not get the final word
