# Storm & Me Official — Product Roadmap

## Vision
Storm & Me is not only an author website. It is an interactive creative home where visitors can see and hear the storm, find hope after it, discover books and music that meet them where they are, and leave feeling lighter.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and Trust
- [x] Cinematic storm homepage
- [x] Rain, lightning, thunder, sunlight, haze, and subtle post-storm rainbow
- [x] Separate rain and slow-piano controls
- [x] Remove duplicate audio components and lock one source of truth
- [x] Add cache recovery and deployment build marker
- [ ] Remove all demo purchase behavior and use honest retailer links
- [ ] Replace placeholder social and YouTube destinations
- [ ] Verify every book cover and Amazon link
- [ ] Complete mobile and accessibility audit

## Phase 2 — Bookstore Experience
- [ ] Organize books into emotional healing, children’s, career/life, and fiction shelves
- [ ] Clearly label series order for The Last Block on Earth Books One and Two
- [ ] Feature The Storm in Me on the homepage
- [ ] Give every book a rich landing page with description, author note, trailer when available, quotes, reviews, sample chapter when supplied, and Amazon purchase links
- [ ] Add Coming Soon shelf for Let It Go, Little Bear and UNHAPPY
- [ ] Add email notification workflow for future releases

## Phase 3 — Author and Story
- [ ] Finish the Meet Willy Will author page with the real portrait, biography, creative journey, and links
- [ ] Expand The Story Behind the Storm into an honest long-form narrative
- [ ] Add behind-the-book stories and writing-process content

## Phase 4 — Music and Video
- [ ] Add verified Willy Will streaming links for YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, TikTok, and other platforms
- [ ] Add real song covers, descriptions, lyrics, stories behind the songs, and playable previews where rights allow
- [ ] Add official YouTube channel and video embeds
- [ ] Add documentaries, book trailers, lyric videos, and behind-the-scenes content

## Phase 5 — Community and Growth
- [ ] Connect newsletter to Constant Contact or another persistent email provider
- [ ] Create welcome email and release/newsletter templates
- [ ] Add testimonials only from real readers
- [ ] Add contact routing and creator inquiries
- [ ] Add analytics events for book clicks, music plays, newsletter signups, and retailer conversions

## Phase 6 — SEO and Launch
- [ ] Per-page titles, descriptions, Open Graph images, and canonical URLs
- [ ] Book, Person, MusicAlbum, MusicRecording, VideoObject, and Organization structured data
- [ ] XML sitemap and robots.txt
- [ ] Google Search Console verification and indexing checks
- [ ] Performance, Core Web Vitals, mobile, and accessibility pass
- [ ] Connect stormandmeofficial.com to the approved Vercel production deployment
- [ ] Launch checklist and announcement campaign

## Signature Experiences
- Rotating homepage encouragement such as “Broken is not your final chapter.”
- Storm mode with gentle rain and occasional thunder.
- After-the-storm mode with sunlight, a natural distant rainbow, and slow peaceful piano.
- A bookstore that feels like walking through a story rather than scrolling through a product grid.
- Every element should support the message: the storm does not get the final word.
