# I AM — Domain and Trademark Risk Review

**Prepared for:** Storm And Me LLC  
**Checked:** August 1, 2026  
**Status:** Preliminary business-risk review — **not legal advice or trademark clearance**

## Executive decision

The product may continue internally as **I AM — working title**, but public launch branding, paid domain acquisition, app-store naming, and trademark filing are on hold until two things are documented:

1. Registrar-level proof of ownership and renewal terms for any domain Storm And Me LLC intends to rely on.
2. A professional U.S. trademark clearance review covering the exact mark, logo, classes, software/services description, related phrases, and common-law use.

The current evidence does **not** support saying that Storm And Me LLC owns `iamhim.ai` or `iamher.ai`, and it does **not** support saying that I AM, I AM HER, or I AM HIM is cleared for launch.

## Preliminary trademark findings

### `I AM`

The phrase is a crowded trademark field. Official USPTO Trademark Trial and Appeal Board records show repeated proceedings and multiple marks involving `I AM`, including activity by i.am.symbolic, LLC/William Adams and other owners. This does not automatically prohibit every use, but it creates a meaningful likelihood of search, classification, and conflict complexity.

**Preliminary risk:** **High** for using `I AM` alone as the primary consumer software/app brand without attorney review.

### `I AM HER`

Official USPTO records show an existing `I AM HER` registration, Registration No. 5496385, with Section 8 and 15 acceptance/acknowledgment, plus additional proceedings involving other `I AM HER` marks. Combined with the unavailable `.ai` domain, this is a strong warning against treating `I AM HER` as a cleared standalone public brand.

**Preliminary risk:** **High to very high** for a standalone public lane, domain, or product name without attorney review.

### `I AM HIM`

The preliminary official-record search did not produce enough evidence to declare the phrase clear or blocked. Absence from these limited search results is not clearance; similar marks, common-law users, app-store names, domains, and relevant international classes still require review.

**Preliminary risk:** **Unresolved**; professional search required.

## Safe interim naming decision

Until clearance is completed:

- Internal documents and the review prototype use **I AM — working title**.
- `Him`, `Her`, and `Becoming` remain internal experience-lane descriptors, not separate businesses or promised public trademarks.
- No public campaign, paid app listing, preorder, domain-forwarding campaign, merchandise, or large logo rollout should rely on the working title.
- Public legal/support pages may remain available for existing technical readiness, but they must not be promoted as evidence of trademark ownership or launch approval.
- Product architecture remains one app and one backend, so a future brand change does not require rebuilding the product.
