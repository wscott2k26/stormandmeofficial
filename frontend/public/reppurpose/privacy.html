RepPurpose Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information RepPurpose uses to personalize workouts, meal logging, scanning, progress, coaching preferences, and account features.

Effective August 19, 2026 · Storm And Me LLC

Who operates RepPurpose

RepPurpose is operated by Storm And Me LLC. RepPurpose is a fitness and wellness-planning application for adults. It provides personalized workout planning, progress tracking, meal logging, camera-assisted analysis, and optional sound, haptic, and voice-coaching features.

Account and profile information

When you create and use an account, RepPurpose may process your account email and the fitness information you choose to provide, such as age, height, weight, goals, experience level, available workout time, workout schedule, disclosed limitations, and coaching preferences. These details are used to tailor the experience you request.

Workout and meal information

RepPurpose may store user-owned workout history, completed workout details, and meal entries that you choose to confirm and save. Nutrition and image-based food estimates can be uncertain and are presented as estimates rather than medical or laboratory measurements.

Camera, photos, and your permission

Camera or photo access is requested only when you choose a feature that needs it, such as scanning your environment or analyzing a meal. After you select a photo, RepPurpose identifies OpenAI as the analysis recipient and asks for your explicit permission before sending that photo. Your consent resets when you choose a different photo. Only after you agree is the selected image sent through a protected RepPurpose server function to OpenAI for the specific analysis you requested. RepPurpose does not save the selected analysis photo to your RepPurpose account.

OpenAI-assisted analysis

RepPurpose uses OpenAI for the environment-scan and meal-photo analysis features. When you explicitly consent for a selected photo, RepPurpose may send that image and the request context needed to produce the requested analysis to OpenAI. If you do not consent, RepPurpose does not send that selected photo for AI analysis. AI results can be incomplete or wrong. RepPurpose is not a physician, dietitian, physical therapist, or diagnostic service, and its estimates and coaching are not medical care.

Sound, haptics, and voice coaching

Sound effects, device haptics, and text-to-speech coaching are optional controls in your RepPurpose profile. The current app configuration does not request microphone access for these playback features. You can turn sound, haptics, and voice coaching on or off from the You screen.

How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your signed-in session.
  • Personalize workouts and coaching to the profile and preferences you provide.
  • Save workouts, confirmed meal entries, and progress you choose to keep.
  • Perform scan or meal analysis that you explicitly request and consent to.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve RepPurpose.
  • Process support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

Service providers

RepPurpose uses infrastructure and platform services including Supabase for authentication, protected server functions, and user-owned application data; OpenAI for the photo-analysis features described above; and Apple, Google, Expo, and applicable device or app-store services. Storm And Me LLC requires service providers that receive RepPurpose user data to handle that data with the same or an equivalent level of protection described in this policy and required by applicable privacy obligations.

Sale and advertising use of fitness data

Storm And Me LLC does not sell your RepPurpose fitness profile, workout history, meal entries, or private scan content. Those categories are not used by Storm And Me LLC to build third-party advertising profiles.

Security and account separation

RepPurpose uses authenticated access controls for user-owned fitness profiles, workouts, and meal entries. The production database is configured so normal signed-in application access is limited to the authenticated user's own rows. Server-only administrative credentials are not embedded in the mobile application.

Your deletion controls

Signed-in users can initiate permanent account deletion inside RepPurpose from the You screen. If the app is no longer installed or accessible, you can also submit an external deletion request from the Storm And Me website. Deleting the authenticated RepPurpose account removes the user-owned fitness profile, workouts, and meal entries associated with that account through the application's deletion workflow. Limited records may be retained only when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or transaction-record obligations.

Retention

Account and user-owned RepPurpose data are retained while needed to provide the service and until deleted through available account controls, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational retention requirements. Public deletion-request records are retained only as reasonably necessary to verify, process, and document the request.

Adults

RepPurpose is currently intended for adults age 18 or older. It is not designed as a children's fitness or social application.

Changes to this policy

If RepPurpose materially changes its account system, data flows, AI providers, analytics, advertising technology, or other privacy-relevant features, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions about RepPurpose, use the Storm & Me Official contact page. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, government identification documents, or detailed medical records through ordinary support messages.
