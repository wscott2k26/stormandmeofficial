RepPurpose · Account & data deletion

Delete your RepPurpose account.

RepPurpose gives you two deletion paths. Signed-in users can delete directly inside the app. If you no longer have the app installed, use the verified web request below. We do not ask for your password on this page.

Fastest: delete inside RepPurpose

  1. Open RepPurpose and sign in.
  2. Open You.
  3. Go to the Account section.
  4. Choose Delete account and confirm.

That signed-in flow verifies your session before deleting the authenticated account.

What the deletion covers

  • Your RepPurpose authentication account.
  • Your saved fitness profile and preferences.
  • Your user-owned workout history.
  • Your user-owned meal entries.

Records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations may be kept only as required.

Request account deletion on the web

Enter the email used for your RepPurpose account. This public form creates a deletion request; it does not expose whether an account exists. Storm And Me LLC may contact you to verify ownership before completing a destructive request.

Never send a password, authentication code, payment credential, medical record, or private photo through this form.
