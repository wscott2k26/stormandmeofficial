FLUME: Headgate Wars Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

FLUME: Headgate Wars is published by Storm And Me LLC. FLUME is an offline single-player strategy game. The current release does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, deck choices, card levels and upgrades, Mastery progress, daily and mission state, and music, sound-effect, and haptic settings are stored locally on the user's device.

Data sent off device

The current release does not send personal information, gameplay activity, location, contacts, photos, files, financial information, health information, or device identifiers to Storm And Me servers. The current release does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Accounts and purchases

The current release does not require account creation and does not contain in-app purchases.

Permissions

The current Android release requests vibration access so the game can provide optional haptic feedback during play.

Deleting local game data

Users can reset locally stored progress from the game's Settings > Reset option. Users can also clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app.

Children

FLUME is not intentionally directed to children under 13 in this release.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes the game's data practices, online features, advertising, analytics, purchases, or account functionality, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
