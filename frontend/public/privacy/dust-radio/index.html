Dust Radio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Dust Radio is developed and published by Storm And Me Studios, operated by Storm And Me LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how Dust Radio handles information when you use the app.

1. No account required

Dust Radio does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its core features. We do not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, password, or social-media profile to listen to music in the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Features such as favorites, listening history, playback preferences, and similar app settings may be stored locally on your device. This locally stored information is used to provide the app's features and is not intentionally uploaded to Dust Radio servers.

3. Audio files you choose

Dust Radio can allow you to select compatible audio files that you already have access to on your device. The app uses the file you select for playback. Dust Radio does not intentionally upload your personal audio files to Storm And Me Studios servers.

4. Online audio and network requests

Dust Radio may connect to online services to retrieve public-domain recordings, creator-licensed audio, catalog information, artwork, or other app content. Like most internet services, those requests may expose ordinary technical information such as an IP address, device or browser headers, request time, and requested resource to the hosting or content provider. Such providers may process this information under their own privacy policies.

5. Technical logs

Hosting systems used to operate Dust Radio may temporarily record limited technical information, such as request times, error details, IP addresses, or device/network metadata, for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. We do not use these technical logs to build advertising profiles.

6. Advertising and tracking

Dust Radio does not include advertising SDKs and does not use cross-app tracking for targeted advertising. Storm And Me Studios does not sell personal information collected through Dust Radio.

7. Sensitive permissions and device access

Dust Radio may request only the device access needed for app features, such as selecting an audio file and supporting background media playback or media controls. Granting access through a system file picker does not give Dust Radio unrestricted access to all files on your device.

8. Children's privacy

Dust Radio is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through the app.

9. Data retention

Information stored locally by Dust Radio generally remains on your device until it is cleared by the app, removed by you, or deleted when the app is uninstalled, depending on your operating system. Temporary technical server logs may be retained only as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled by the service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-party services

Dust Radio may link to or retrieve content from third-party archives, hosting providers, or creator-licensed sources. Storm And Me Studios does not control the privacy practices of third parties. Their own policies apply when their services are used.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Dust Radio evolves, legal requirements change, or app features are added. The current version will remain available at this URL, and the "Last updated" date will be changed when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about Dust Radio, contact Storm And Me Studios through the official Storm And Me contact page:

https://stormandmeofficial.com/contact
