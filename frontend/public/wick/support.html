Need the fastest fix?
  • Glow purchase missing: open Wick → Glow → Restore purchase.
  • Backup: Glow → Export my wick.
  • Restore a backup: Glow → Import.
  • Wick works without an account and keeps core journal data locally.

Glow

Glow is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use the same Google Play or Apple account that made the purchase, then tap Restore purchase.

Data and backups

Your Wick logs and notes stay in local app storage. If you change devices, use Glow's export/import tools to move a backup you control.

Reset

Reset is a simple paced-breathing feature for personal reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor a medical condition.
