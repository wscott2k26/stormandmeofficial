Wick Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026 · Storm And Me LLC

Short version: Wick is designed to work without an account. Your social-energy logs and optional notes stay in local app storage unless you deliberately export a backup. Storm And Me LLC does not sell your personal data.

Information Wick stores

Wick stores your lantern level, check-ins, optional notes, preferences, and related app settings locally on your device. The app does not require you to create an account.

Information sent to Storm And Me

Wick does not send your journal history, check-ins, or optional notes to Storm And Me servers. Version 1.0.0 does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Glow purchases

Glow is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or the App Store on iOS. Wick receives the store entitlement result needed to unlock Glow. Storm And Me does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Export and import

If Glow is unlocked, you may choose to export a Wick JSON backup or import one. Export occurs only when you initiate it. You control where the exported file is saved or shared.

Device backups

Android application backup is disabled by Wick. Device-transfer or operating-system behavior outside Wick may be controlled by your platform provider. Wick's explicit export/import tools remain under your control.

Children

Wick is a general lifestyle and reflection app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Wick.

Medical disclaimer

Wick is a personal reflection and wellness tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, or substitute for professional care.

Changes

If Wick later adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other data collection, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before that functionality is released.

Contact

Wick is published by Storm And Me LLC. For support or privacy questions, use the Wick support page.
